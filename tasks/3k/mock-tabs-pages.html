Paged tab strip: one row, every cell the same width, the current tab underlined. Move with [/] or Alt-←/Alt-→ (Ctrl-N/P in the real thing). Inside a page only the underline moves; crossing a page border flips the row. The edge buttons show how many tabs are on the other pages and the most urgent marker among them; click to flip.

Names appear only if every tab fits with its name. Resize the window, or change the tab count:

Markers: ◆ needs a look, ✗ error, ● working, ✓ done.
